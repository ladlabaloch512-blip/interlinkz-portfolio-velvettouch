Bridal and Party Packages

Your day, beautifully handled. Bridal packages include a full trial session before the day, so there is nothing to worry about when it matters. Tell us your date early, wedding season weekends fill fast.

About the trial session

Every Bridal Signature Package starts with a trial session before the day. We try the full makeup look and hairstyle, adjust them with you, and note everything down, so the day itself is calm and unhurried.

Bridal packages take a small booking amount to hold your date, which we explain fully before you confirm anything. Party Makeup needs no trial and can be booked like any other appointment.

Ask about your date

Tell us the package you are interested in and your date, and we will reply with availability and the next steps.
