About Velvet Touch Salon and Spa

The salon its founders always looked for and never found.

Our story

Velvet Touch Salon and Spa was created as the salon its founders always looked for and never found: skilled people, honest prices on the wall, and a room that lowers your shoulders the moment you walk in. We offer hair, skin, nail and spa treatments under one roof, and we sell the same professional products we use, so your care does not stop at our door.

We do not chase every trend, and we will never sell you a treatment your hair or skin does not need. Good work, a calm room and straight answers: that is the whole idea, and it seems to suit our clients very well.

What we promise

Three simple promises, kept every day.

Hygiene, without shortcuts

Tools are sterilised before use each day, towels and linens are single client use, and treatment rooms are cleaned between every appointment.

A team approach

We work as one team, not as competing chairs. Whoever is best for your hair or skin looks after you, and every stylist works to the same standard.

Products we stand behind

We only sell what we use on our own clients. If a product is on our shelf, it has earned its place, and we will happily tell you how to get the best from it at home.
